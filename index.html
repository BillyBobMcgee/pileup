All your deadlines on one page.

  1. Paste your course outlines or your Google Classroom page. It finds the dates and what each thing is worth.
  2. See which weeks have too much due, and which easy week to get ahead in.
  3. Add new stuff as it comes up, like "chem quiz fri", and tick it off when it's done.

Works with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and Outlook.

    A week is marked as a pileup when 3 or more things are due and add up to 20% or more of your grades, or when what is due adds up to 30% or more. Things you've ticked off don't count.

One map for every deadline in every course.

  1. Paste each course outline on the left. Pileup reads the dates and what each thing is worth.
  2. See the weeks where too much lands at once, and the quiet week before to get ahead in.
  3. Save it all to your calendar with reminders.

Works with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and Outlook.

    A week is marked as a pileup when 3 or more things are due and add up to 20% or more of your grades, or when what is due adds up to 30% or more.
